## 📸 Adding Images

### Profile Image
1. Add your profile picture to `public/profile.jpg` or `profile.png`
2. Uncomment the Image component in `components/Hero.tsx` (line ~50)

### Project Images
1. Add project screenshots to the `public` folder:
   - `project-ecommerce.jpg`
   - `project-task.jpg`
   - `project-dashboard.jpg`
   - `project-blog.jpg`
   - `project-chat.jpg`
   - `project-weather.jpg`
2. Uncomment the Image components in `components/Projects.tsx` (line ~120)
